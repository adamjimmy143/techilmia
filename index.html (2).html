Frequently asked questions

Questions schools ask before choosing digital management software.

Clear answers help school leaders understand how a modern school management system can fit everyday academic and administrative work.

What is a school management system?

A school management system is software that brings student records, attendance, timetable, examinations, fees, staff and administrative workflows into one connected environment.

Why use school ERP software?

School ERP software can reduce duplicated data entry, improve access to information and give different departments a focused workspace based on their responsibilities.

Can students and teachers use the same system?

Yes. A role-based design can give teachers, students, administrators and departments different dashboard views while keeping approved information connected.
